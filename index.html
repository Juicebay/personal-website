About

I study empirical microeconomics with a focus on urban and environmental economics. My current research looks at market design under affirmative action, the economics of rental markets, and how competition between AI models changes quality. Along the way I work with R, Python, Stata, and ArcGIS.

Outside research, I write about what I'm learning, collect small adventures, and believe good ideas deserve clear explanations. You can reach me at xingming.li@mail.concordia.ca.
